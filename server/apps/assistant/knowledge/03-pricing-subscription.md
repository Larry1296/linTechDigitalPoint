# Pricing and subscriptions

## How much does {platform} cost?
There are two monthly plans, paid by M-Pesa:
- **Basic: usually KES 500 per month.** Products & stock, services & jobs, point of sale and receipts, customers and credit, expenses, and M-Pesa agency.
- **Pro: usually KES 1,000 per month.** Everything in Basic, plus profit & loss, balance sheet and other reports, shelves & locations, staff accounts and roles, and more than one branch.

The price depends on the kind of business: a small kibanda can pay less than a hardware shop for the same plan. Your own price is shown on the **Subscription** page, and set-up shows it once you choose your kind of business. Changing the kind of business in Settings does not change the price; ask the platform team if your business has changed.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every new business gets its **first month free** on the plan chosen during set-up, so you can set up your business before paying. On Basic you will see an invitation to upgrade to Pro. Paying during the free month keeps the free days: the paid month starts when the free month ends.

## How do I pay, renew or upgrade?
Open **Subscription** in the menu (only the business owner or an administrator can). Enter the Safaricom number to pay from, then click the plan. Confirm, and an M-Pesa prompt appears on that phone: enter your M-Pesa PIN. The page updates by itself once the payment is received. Renewing the same plan adds a month to your current end date. Upgrading to Pro applies straight away, and the unused days you already paid for are converted at your Pro price.

## How do I know how long is left?
Every workspace page shows how many days are left, and the top bar shows your plan and days remaining. A reminder appears in the last 5 days.

## What happens when my subscription ends?
Your records are kept and you can still sign in, record counter sales and look at your products, services and customers. Everything else is paused until you renew: reports and accounting, expenses, stock receiving and adjustments, shelves, staff, branches and the M-Pesa agency. Renew on the Subscription page and everything comes back straight away.

## I paid but my subscription did not activate.
M-Pesa usually confirms within a minute. If money was deducted and the subscription is still not active after a few minutes, contact the {platform} team with the M-Pesa confirmation message, and they can record the payment for you.
