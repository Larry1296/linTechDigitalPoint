# What {platform} is

## What is {platform}?
{platform} is an online business management platform for small and growing businesses. It brings your products and stock, counter sales, services, payments, expenses, staff and reports into one private workspace that belongs to your business. You use it in a web browser on a computer, laptop, tablet or phone; there is nothing to install.

## Who is {platform} for?
It is built for businesses that sell and serve, especially in East Africa: retail shops, groceries and mama mboga stalls, wholesalers and distributors, electronics and phone-accessory shops, cyber cafés and printing shops, service businesses (salons, repairs, tailoring, cleaning), restaurants, cafés and food kiosks, pharmacies and agrovets, hardware and building-supply shops, and M-Pesa agents. Mixed businesses that sell products and services side by side are supported too.

## What can I do with {platform}? (features and modules)
You choose the tools (modules) your business needs, and can change them later in Settings:
- **Products & stock**: products, prices, categories, brands, receiving stock, stock counts and adjustments.
- **Point of sale**: sell at the counter, take payments and send receipts by WhatsApp, email or print.
- **Shelves & locations**: map your shop areas, racks, levels and compartments so anyone can find an item fast.
- **Services**: a service price list, jobs and quick services such as printing, typing or repairs.
- **Customers**: customer records, credit sales and balances owed.
- **Expenses**: record money spent, such as rent, transport and electricity.
- **M-Pesa agency**: agent float, deposits, withdrawals, commission and reconciliation.
- **Reports**: profit and loss, balance sheet, sales and expense trends.
- **Branches**: run more than one shop or location.
- **Staff**: give each person their own sign-in and a role.

## Which countries and currencies are supported?
You pick your country, currency and time zone when you set up the business. Built-in choices include Kenya (KES), Uganda (UGX), Tanzania (TZS), Rwanda (RWF), Burundi (BIF), South Sudan (SSP), Ethiopia (ETB), Somalia (SOS), DR Congo (CDF), Zambia (ZMW), Malawi (MWK), Nigeria (NGN), Ghana (GHS), South Africa (ZAR), United Arab Emirates (AED), India (INR), the United Kingdom (GBP) and the United States (USD); EUR and CNY are also available as currencies. The currency is fixed once you start recording money, so past records stay correct.

## Do I need special hardware?
No. Any device with a modern web browser works. A barcode scanner that types into the browser works for searching products at the counter and in product lookup, and receipts can be printed on a normal or receipt printer, or saved as PDF.
