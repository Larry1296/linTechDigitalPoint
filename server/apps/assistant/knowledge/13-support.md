# Contact and support

## How do I contact the {platform} team or get support?
{contact}
For questions about pricing, plans, renewing a subscription or anything this assistant cannot answer, please contact the team directly.

## What can this assistant help with?
This assistant answers general questions about {platform}: what it does, how to get started, features, pricing and plans, and how to use each part of the workspace. It cannot see or change your business records, reset your password for you, or make changes to your account or subscription.

## Can you tell me my sales, profit or what my business is worth?
This public chat cannot see your business records. [Sign in](/login) to your workspace: the **Dashboard** shows **What the business is worth** (what it owns, what it owes and how it changes over time) along with your sales and profit, and the **business assistant** inside your workspace can answer questions about your own figures.
