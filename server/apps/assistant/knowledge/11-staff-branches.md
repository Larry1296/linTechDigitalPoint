# Staff, roles and branches

## How do I add staff?
In **Settings**, use **Add a staff member**: enter their name, email, a username and a starting password, and choose a role. Each person gets their own sign-in, and the role decides what they can see and do. You can change a person's role or suspend their access later. Your plan may limit the number of users.

## Which staff roles are there?
- **Owner**: full control of the business (the person who created it).
- **Admin**: full access to everything in the business.
- **Manager**: runs day-to-day operations across products, stock, sales, services, M-Pesa and reports.
- **Cashier**: makes sales, takes payments and adds customers; sees products but not costs or profit.
- **Inventory**: manages products, categories, receiving and stock.
- **Accountant**: works with expenses and accounting reports.
- **Service attendant**: handles the service price list, jobs and quick services only.
- **Read only**: can view records but cannot change anything and cannot see profit.

## Can I run more than one shop (branches)?
Yes, with the Branches module. Add a branch in Settings. Each branch keeps its own stock, sales and cash, and reports can be viewed per branch or for the whole business. Staff can be assigned to specific branches as branch admin, branch manager or branch staff. The main branch can be edited but not deleted. Your plan may limit the number of branches.
