# Security, privacy and your account

## Is my business data private and secure?
Yes. Each business has its own private workspace: its products, locations, sales, transactions and reports belong to that business, and other subscribers cannot see them. Inside the business, each staff member only sees what their role allows. The workspace locks when you press Lock, the private pages (M-Pesa and investments) lock by themselves after 5 minutes untouched, and sign-in attempts are rate limited.

## I forgot my password. What do I do?
Go to [Forgot password?](/forgot-password), enter your account email and request reset instructions. Follow the link you receive to choose a new password, then sign in again.

## How do I change my password?
Signed in, open **Settings** and use **Change your password**. For your safety you are then signed out on all devices and asked to sign in again.

## Why was I signed out or asked to unlock?
The workspace never locks for being idle: it stays open for 12 hours after you sign in, so you can answer a customer at once. It locks only when you press Lock workspace, and opens again with your lock PIN (4 to 6 digits). After 12 hours, or after five wrong PINs, it asks for your password, which starts a fresh 12 hours.

## What are private pages and their PINs?
M-Pesa and investments are private pages. They open with your lock PIN, or with a PIN of their own if you give them one in Settings > Lock PIN and private pages; one PIN opens every page that shares it. A private page locks again after 5 minutes untouched, when you press Lock now, or when the workspace locks. Forgot a PIN? Your sign-in password opens the private pages, and you can set a new PIN in Settings.

## How do I switch between businesses?
If your account belongs to more than one business, you choose the business after signing in and can switch to another business you are authorized to access from your workspace.

## How do I add or change my logo?
In **Settings → Business logo**, upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image (up to 5 MB). A plain white, grey or black background is removed automatically, and the logo is cropped and resized so it shows clearly on your dashboard and on the receipts your customers receive. For the best result upload the original, high-resolution logo file.

## Can I change my business name, currency or time zone?
Business details, modules and time zone can be changed in Settings. The currency is fixed once you start recording money, so past records stay correct.
