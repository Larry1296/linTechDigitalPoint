# Products, categories and brands

## How do I add a product?
Open **Products** in your workspace and click **Add Product**. Choose whether it is a stock item or a billable service, give it a name, category and optional brand, set the selling price and, for stock items, the buying price, the quantity you already have and where it is kept. {platform} suggests a SKU (stock code) from the brand and name; you can use the suggestion or type your own. You can also scan or type the barcode.

## What are variants?
A product can have variants, such as colours, sizes or models (for example a phone cover in black, blue and clear). Each variant has its own SKU, barcode, price, stock and shelf. A product with no options simply has one "Standard" variant.

## How do categories and brands work?
Categories group products the way your staff recognise them, and can have sub-categories (for example Phones > Chargers). Brands are optional. You can manage both from the Categories and Brands pages, or create them while adding a product. The Products list is grouped by category.

## What are the lowest price and restock level?
- **Lowest price** is a guide for bargaining at the counter. Staff can agree any price up to the selling price, even below the lowest price, but never below what the item cost to buy. Sales below the lowest price are noted in the audit log for the owner to review.
- **Restock level** (minimum stock) is the quantity at which a product is flagged "restock", so you can see what is running low.

## Can I add product photos?
Yes. Open a product and use **Photos** to add display photos for the product or for a specific variant.

## How do I edit, hide or delete a product?
Open the product and click **Edit** (product details) or **Edit variant** (price, SKU, barcode, lowest price, restock level). A product entered by mistake can be deleted only if nothing has been sold, received or moved for it. Otherwise untick **Active** and save to hide it; hidden products keep their history.

## How do I find a product quickly?
Use the search box on the Products page, or the **Product lookup** page, and type a name, SKU or barcode, or scan the barcode. Product lookup shows the selling price, available stock and exactly which shelf it is on.
