# Chama (savings group)

## What is the Chama tool?
A Pro tool for a business owner who is also the treasurer (or another leader) of a chama: a group whose members give an agreed amount every day, week or month and usually take turns receiving the money (merry-go-round). Set it up from the dashboard (**Set up a chama**) or the **Chama** menu. The chama's money belongs to its members, so it is kept apart from the shop: it never shows in the shop's sales, profit, money check or reports.

## How do I record a member's payment?
Open the chama, find the member on the board and press **Record payment**. Enter the amount, the date it was paid and how (cash, M-Pesa with its code, bank or other). The money pays the member's oldest unpaid contributions first, then any fines, then contributions ahead.

## How do fines work?
Once a contribution's deadline (its date plus the grace days) has passed, a member who has not paid it in full is fined once. If the money was paid on time but recorded after the deadline, the fine is cancelled by itself. A fine can be waived with a reason under **Fines & excuses**, for example when the member was sick.

## Can I reverse (undo) a chama payment recorded by mistake?
Yes. Open the chama, go to **Money record**, find the payment under **Money paid in** and press **Reverse**. Give a short reason, such as "entered twice". The payment is undone as if it never happened: the chama no longer holds that money, the member owes what it paid again (contributions and fines), and a fine for paying late comes back where the deadline has passed. The reversed payment leaves the list but stays on record behind **Show reversed**, with who reversed it and why. Only the owner, administrators and anyone allowed to change the chama can reverse.

## Why can't I reverse a payment?
If the money it brought in has already been given out to a member, reversing it would leave the chama holding less than nothing. Reverse that payout first under **Money given out**, then the payment. A payout is reversed the same way, latest turn first.

## Can a member pay late with an excuse?
Yes. Press **Excuse** on the board, enter the reason and the date they promise to pay by. They are fined only if they have not paid in full by that date.
