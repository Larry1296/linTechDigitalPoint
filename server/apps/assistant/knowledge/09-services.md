# Services, jobs and cyber café

## Can I sell services as well as products?
Yes. With the Services module you keep a service price list (for example printing, typing, scanning, repairs, hair styling or tailoring) and charge for services alongside products. The price actually charged is kept on every completed job or transaction.

## How do service jobs work?
Use **New job / Quick service** on the service desk. A quick service (like printing a few pages) is recorded and charged immediately. A longer job (like a repair) can be opened, updated and completed later. Service attendants can be given a role that only lets them handle services and jobs.

## How does a customer pay for a service, and can they pay later?
Services are paid at the same checkout as the point of sale. After choosing what was done (or pressing **Pay & complete** on a job), press **Charge**, then take cash, the till, an M-Pesa prompt, bank, or a mix. To let a customer pay later, choose or add the customer before charging; **Owed** then leaves the rest on their account, with an optional due date, and it shows with the business's other money owed by customers.

## Can a service use stock, like paper or spare parts?
Yes. You can record the physical materials a service uses, for example paper for printing or a spare part for a repair, and they are taken out of stock so your inventory and costs stay accurate.

## Is {platform} good for a cyber café or printing shop?
Yes. Choose "Cyber café & printing" during set-up and the Services, Products & stock, Point of sale, Expenses and Reports tools are switched on for you. The Cyber desk gives you a service price list, jobs and quick services, with materials such as paper tracked in stock.
