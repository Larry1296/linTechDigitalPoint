# Customers, expenses and reports

## How do I keep customer records?
With the Customers module you can save customers (name and contact), link sales to them, sell on credit and see each customer's balance. Reports list how much customers owe you.

## How do I record expenses?
Use **Record money spent** in Expenses. Enter the amount, category (for example rent, transport, electricity), which part of the business it belongs to, and whether it was paid from cash, bank or mobile money. You can also record unpaid bills. Expenses appear in your profit and loss.

## What reports does {platform} have?
- **Dashboard**: today at a glance: sales, payments, stock and orders needing attention.
- **Profit and loss**: sales, gross profit, operating expenses and net profit or loss for any date range, with **Explain profit** showing how profit is calculated.
- **Balance sheet**: where the business stands today: cash, bank and mobile money, stock value, what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers.
- **Cash flow** and **expense breakdown** by category.
- **Analytics**: sales and expense trends.
- **Posted accounting reports**: trial balance and balanced journal reports for your accountant.
Reports can be viewed for the whole business or per branch.

## Can I hide profit and cost figures from staff?
Yes. Access to cost and profit information follows each staff member's role, so cashiers and read-only staff do not see buying prices or profit unless you allow it.

## How does {platform} calculate profit?
Sales are matched with the actual cost of the stock sold (oldest stock first), then operating expenses are subtracted. Stock without a buying price is flagged as uncosted, because its profit cannot be known until you enter the cost.
