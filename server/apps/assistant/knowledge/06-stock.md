# Stock, receiving and shelves

## How do I receive stock from a supplier?
Use **Receive stock** (or **Quick receive** for a fast single item). Choose the product, enter the quantity received and the buying price for one, the supplier and the reference (invoice or delivery note number). You can add delivery and other costs, which are spread over the items, and choose exactly which shelf the stock goes to. Every receipt is kept, with its costs, in **Recent Receipts**. If the stock was already yours before you started recording, enter it with **Stock I Already Have** (or the product import), not through Receive stock: Receive stock records a purchase.

## How do I correct a mistake on a received delivery?
Open **Recent Receipts** (in the stock section) and click the receipt. A saved receipt is never edited away; it is corrected with a permanent record. To fix a wrong buying price, wrong item or wrong quantity on a line, click **Correct receipt line**. What is owed to the supplier follows the corrected price.

## The app says I owe a supplier, but I don't. How do I clear it?
A delivery saved with "No — owe the supplier (pay later)" counts as money owed to that supplier until it is cleared. Open **Recent Receipts** (in the stock section); a receipt that still owes shows "owed" in the list. Click it, and the **Supplier** box shows what was bought, paid and is still owed, with the right fix for each case. The stock itself is never returned or entered again:
- **I paid the supplier** (now, or at the time but it was not recorded): click **I paid [supplier]**, enter the amount, the date you really paid (the delivery date if you paid on arrival) and where the money came from: the shop cash drawer, M-Pesa (Lipa na M-Pesa till), bank, an investment (MMF), or **My own money (not from the shop)**. Paying from your own money leaves the drawer and till unchanged and counts as money you put into the business.
- **The stock was already mine** (I had it before I started recording, and entered it through Receive stock instead of as opening stock): click **This stock was already mine**. The receipt becomes opening stock: nothing is owed, it no longer counts as stock bought, and the items, shelves and buying prices stay as they are. This is only offered when the receipt has no payment, return or delivery cost.
Both can be undone from the same box (**Undo** beside a payment, or **It was a purchase after all**). Recording a payment does not change what the business is worth: the debt and the money paid out cancel each other. Marking stock as already owned does not change it either: the debt becomes the owner's stake in the business.

## How is stock cost tracked?
Every delivery is kept as its own stock lot with its own cost, and sales use the oldest stock first (first in, first out). That is how {platform} calculates the real cost and profit of each sale.

## How do I count stock or fix a wrong quantity (stock adjustment)?
Open **Adjust stock**, choose the exact variant and the compartment, and enter the physical count you made (the total you counted, not the difference). Choose a reason such as physical count variance, damaged, lost, data-entry correction or found stock. If the count is higher than the system, choose an existing stock lot or enter the unit cost of the found stock. Posted adjustments are permanent audit records; fix a mistake with another adjustment. The page tells you what is still missing if the post button is disabled.

## What are shelves and locations?
With the Shelves & locations module you map your shop: shop areas, racks, levels and compartments, and which categories each rack holds. Every stock quantity is then tied to an exact compartment, so staff can find items fast and stock counts are per shelf. Set this up in **Shelf management**.

## What does "Not on a shelf yet" (unplaced stock) mean?
Stock that arrived without a shelf, for example imported opening stock, sits in a holding location. Open the product and click **Place on a shelf**, choose the compartment and quantity, and move it. The stock keeps its original cost.

## How do I know what is running low?
Products at or below their restock level are marked "restock" in the Products list and on the product page, and the dashboard summarises stock. Set each product's restock level when adding or editing it.
