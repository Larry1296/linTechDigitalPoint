# Point of sale, receipts and refunds

## How do I make a sale at the counter?
Open **Point of Sale**. Tap products to add them (each tap adds one more; the number on the product shows how many are in the sale), use the category buttons to narrow the list, or search by name, item code or barcode. The bar at the bottom shows how many items are in the sale and the total as you go; picking products never interrupts you. When you are done, press **Complete sale** (or F2): the checkout opens over the whole screen. Check the items, change quantities with − and + or agree a price, choose the customer if any, and press **Charge**. Then take the payment (Cash, Till or Paybill, Prompt, Bank, or Owed). For cash, type what the customer gave and the change is worked out. **Add more products** goes back to the products with the sale kept. Stock updates as soon as the sale is completed, and a receipt is produced.

## Which payment methods can I record?
Cash, M-Pesa, bank and other payments, recorded against each sale so your takings and stock stay in one place.

## Can the till send an M-Pesa prompt to the customer's phone?
Yes, if your business has its own Safaricom **till number (Buy Goods)** or **paybill**. Choose M-Pesa, type the customer's number and press **Complete sale & send prompt**: the customer gets a prompt on their phone, enters their M-Pesa PIN, the money goes straight to your till or paybill, and the sale is marked paid with the M-Pesa code by itself. If they cancel or the phone is off, send the prompt again or enter the code from their message by hand.
The business owner sets it up once in **Settings → M-Pesa payments** (it is also offered right after registering a business). You need: your till number and store number (or paybill number); a free Safaricom Daraja account (developer.safaricom.co.ke) with an M-Pesa Express app taken live with your till or paybill; the app's Consumer Key and Consumer Secret; and the Lipa na M-Pesa Online passkey Safaricom emails after Go Live. {platform} checks the keys with Safaricom when you save, and you can send yourself a KES 1 test prompt. A business without a till or paybill records M-Pesa by hand with the code from the customer's message.

## Can customers buy on credit or pay part now?
Yes. Choose **Credit sale or partial payment**, pick or add the customer (a customer is needed if anything is left to pay) and optionally a due date. The unpaid amount becomes the customer's balance, and you can record their payments later from Sales history. Reports show how much customers owe you.

## Can cashiers give a discount or bargain?
Anyone at the till may agree any price up to the selling price. The only hard limit is the buying price: a price below what the item cost is refused (cashiers are not shown the buying price). Each product can have a lowest price as a guide; going below it is allowed and is noted in the audit log for the owner to review. A price above the selling price, or a discount, needs a manager.

## How do customers get their receipt?
After a sale you can print the receipt (on a normal or receipt printer, or save it as PDF), or share it by WhatsApp or email as a private receipt link the customer can open without an account. Receipts show your business logo and details.

## How do I refund or accept a return?
Open **Sales history**, find the sale and choose to return some items or return all goods. You can record a refund now in cash, M-Pesa or bank. Returned goods in good condition go back into stock; damaged items can be kept out of stock and are recorded as a stock loss. Every return and refund is recorded, so the sale's history stays complete.

## Where can I see past sales?
**Sales history** lists completed sales with their payments, what is still owed, returns and refunds. The dashboard shows today at a glance.
