# Hotels, restaurants and lodgings

## Is {platform} good for a hotel (hoteli), restaurant or café?
Yes. Choose "Hotel / restaurant (eatery)" or "Food kiosk & café" during set-up. The **Menu & kitchen** tool is switched on: your menu (tea, chapati, pilau…) with prices, sold at the till like anything else, plus the kitchen's stock. Bottled drinks such as sodas and water are ordinary products counted in stock.

## Do I have to count plates of food in stock?
No. A dish on the menu is made to order, so it always sells at the till without a stock count. What is counted is what you cook with: flour, oil, sugar, meat and so on, received through **Receive stock** like any stock. If a dish runs out for the day, press **Run out** on the Menu page and it leaves the till until you press **Back on**.

## How does the cost of meals reach my profit?
On the **Kitchen** page, record what the kitchen used each day. Either type in what was used, or count what is left and {platform} works out what was used (what the books show less what is left). Its cost, at what you paid for that stock (oldest first), is the cost of the meals sold, so profit is meal sales less food used less your other expenses.

## Is {platform} good for a hotel with rooms, a lodge or a guest house?
Yes. Choose "Hotel with rooms & restaurant" (rooms plus meals and drinks) or "Lodging / guest house" (rooms only). Add your kinds of room with their price a night and your room numbers on **Rooms & prices**. The **Front desk** shows every room: who is in, who arrives and leaves today, which are free and which need cleaning.

## How are room nights charged?
The first night is charged when the guest checks in, and one more each time they are still in after the check-out time (10:00 unless you change it on Rooms & prices). A guest who arrives on Monday afternoon and leaves on Wednesday before 10:00 pays two nights. A guest who leaves early never needs a refund for nights they did not sleep, because nights are only charged as they are stayed.

## Can a guest charge food and drinks to their room?
Yes. At the till, after **Charge**, choose **Room** and pick the guest's room. The sale goes on their bill and is paid when they check out, or from money they paid in advance.

## What about deposits and paying in advance?
Money a guest pays before or during their stay is held for them and used against their bill as nights and other charges are added. At check-out anything not used is given back (cash, M-Pesa or bank). If a booking is cancelled, or the guest never comes, what they paid can be given back or kept as the booking's price.

## How does check-out work?
Open the guest from the Front desk and press **Check out**. Nights are brought up to date first, then you take what they still owe. A manager can let a trusted guest (for example a company) leave owing it on their customer account. The room is then marked as needing cleaning until someone presses **Cleaned**.

## Can I add laundry, an extra bed or other charges?
Yes. On the guest's page, **Add a charge** with what it is for and the amount. It joins their bill with the nights and anything charged from the till.

## How do the restaurant and the rooms show in my books?
A hotel with rooms and a restaurant can be run as two parts, "Rooms" and "Restaurant & bar", each with its own sales, costs and money, and the dashboard combines their profit. Room nights and guest charges are kept as rooms income; meals and drinks as restaurant income.
